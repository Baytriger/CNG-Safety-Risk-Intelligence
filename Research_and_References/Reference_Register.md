# Reference register

Independent portfolio case study (simulated data). Each source was opened between 3 and 4 November 2026. 'Mandatory' means a Nigerian instrument whose applicability to a given licence still needs legal confirmation. Everything else is voluntary guidance or context.

## R01  Petroleum Industry Act, 2021 (Act No. 6 of 2021)
- **Issuer:** Federal Republic of Nigeria
- **Status:** Mandatory: Nigerian statute
- **Date:** Official Gazette vol. 108, no. 142/2021 (assent date not shown on the page used)
- **Link:** https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/ng/national-legislation/petroleum-industry-act-no-6-2021
- **Why relevant:** Establishes the midstream and downstream regulator and its powers of inspection, examination and surveillance of petroleum and gas installations.
- **What was verified:** Catalogue summary read. Full Act text not reviewed; detailed HSE sections were not confirmed.

## R02  Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Safety Regulations, S.I. No. 33 of 2023
- **Issuer:** Made under the PIA 2021; Official Gazette No. 106 of 2023
- **Status:** Mandatory: Nigerian regulation
- **Date:** 2023 (exact commencement date not stated on the page used)
- **Link:** https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/ng/national-legislation/midstream-and-downstream-petroleum-safety-regulations-statutory
- **Why relevant:** Sets safety standards and occupational health requirements for midstream and downstream petroleum and natural gas operations; assigns duties to licensees, permit holders and managers (e.g. fire precaution, risk-based inspection).
- **What was verified:** Catalogue abstract read. The full regulation text was NOT reviewed, so clause-level obligations for CNG sites are not stated in this project and must be confirmed against the gazetted text.

## R03  Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Operations Regulations, S.I. No. 8 of 2023
- **Issuer:** Made under ss. 33, 125(3) and 174(3) PIA 2021; Official Gazette No. 41 of 2023
- **Status:** Mandatory: Nigerian regulation
- **Date:** 2023 (exact commencement date not stated on the page used)
- **Link:** https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/midstream-and-downstream-petroleum-operations-regulations-statutory-instrument-no-8-of-2023-lex-faoc219175/
- **Why relevant:** Governs licensing, permits and the duties of licensees and permit holders, including emergency and health procedures, in the midstream and downstream sector.
- **What was verified:** Catalogue abstract read. Full text not reviewed; applicability to a specific Powergas licence category not assessed.

## R04  Employee's Compensation Act, 2010 (Act No. 13)
- **Issuer:** Federal Republic of Nigeria; administered by NSITF
- **Status:** Mandatory: Nigerian statute
- **Date:** Enacted 17 December 2010; gazetted 20 December 2010
- **Link:** https://nsitf.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ECA-2010.pdf
- **Why relevant:** Requires employers to report work-related injuries to the Board and the nearest NCOSH office within 7 days and fatalities immediately (s.5); sets employer contribution duties (s.33). Used to shape the incident-reporting fields in the simulated log.
- **What was verified:** Act text read (first 100,000 of 102,725 characters). Section numbers quoted above were taken from that text.

## R05  Factories Act (1987 No. 16), as published by PLAC
- **Issuer:** Federal Republic of Nigeria
- **Status:** Mandatory where premises meet the Act's definition of a factory; applicability to CNG sites to be confirmed by counsel
- **Date:** 11 June 1987 (commencement date on the text)
- **Link:** https://www.placng.org/lawsofnigeria/laws/F1.pdf
- **Why relevant:** General safety provisions: confined-space fumes (s.29), explosion prevention from gas, dust or vapour and hot work near flammables (s.30), fire detection and escape (ss.35-36), accident notification (ss.51-53).
- **What was verified:** Text read (first 100,000 of 126,266 characters). The 'Cap F1 LFN 2004' consolidation citation and the definition of 'factory' (s.87) were NOT confirmed from the text read.

## R06  NMDPRA regulations overview, CANDELP Newsletter Vol. 003/2023
- **Issuer:** Commercial and Energy Law Practice (law-firm newsletter, secondary source)
- **Status:** Context only: secondary source
- **Date:** 2023 (no publication date printed)
- **Link:** https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/60fac140de47190a3a85ad65/650a0c65368a76c4c5512fca_NMDPRA%20-%20Regulations%20and%20Updates.pdf
- **Why relevant:** Lists the 2023 NMDPRA regulation set, including Gas Distribution Systems Regulations, Midstream Gas Flare Regulations and the Operations Regulations. Used to show which primary texts a CNG distributor should review.
- **What was verified:** Newsletter read. It does not mention CNG and gives no S.I. numbers. Primary texts must be consulted before any obligation is relied on.

## R07  Nigeria ratifies ILO Conventions on occupational safety and health and on violence and harassment
- **Issuer:** International Labour Organization
- **Status:** International instrument ratified by Nigeria (binds the State; not a direct employer duty)
- **Date:** Ratification deposited 8 November 2022
- **Link:** https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/nigeria-ratifies-ilo-conventions-occupational-safety-and-health-and
- **Why relevant:** Confirms Nigeria ratified C187 (Promotional Framework for OSH, 2006) and C190. Supports the principle of a national OSH system and programme.
- **What was verified:** Page read. It does not mention C155, so no claim is made here about C155 ratification.

## R08  ISO 45001:2018 Occupational health and safety management systems: Requirements with guidance for use
- **Issuer:** International Organization for Standardization
- **Status:** Voluntary: international standard
- **Date:** First edition, March 2018
- **Link:** https://iteh.es/catalog/standards/iso/7af570c3-7e11-4fcc-8650-219c1a48dc03/iso-45001-2018
- **Why relevant:** Basis for the hazard identification, risk assessment, hierarchy of controls, corrective action and performance monitoring structure used here. This project borrows ISO 45001 principles only and does not claim conformity or certification.
- **What was verified:** Edition and date confirmed from a standards-catalogue listing (the ISO.org page could not be retrieved from this environment). The standard itself was not purchased or read in full.

## R09  NFPA 52 Vehicular Natural Gas Fuel Systems Code, 2023 edition
- **Issuer:** National Fire Protection Association
- **Status:** Voluntary: international code (adoption in Nigeria not confirmed)
- **Date:** 2023 edition (publication date not shown on the page used)
- **Link:** https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/nfpa/nfpa522023
- **Why relevant:** Fundamental safeguards against fire and explosion for CNG and LNG vehicle fuel systems, fuelling stations and their storage. Cited as a reference point for fuelling and storage hazards; whether it applies to a mother station or tube-trailer transfer is for a competent person to decide.
- **What was verified:** Catalogue page read for title, edition and scope. The code text itself was not read. A 2026 edition is also listed on the ANSI webstore (publication date not stated there); the edition that applies to any real project must be confirmed.

## R10  Hierarchy of Controls
- **Issuer:** NIOSH, US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
- **Status:** Voluntary: international guidance
- **Date:** Page states last reviewed and updated 28 October 2026
- **Link:** https://cdc.gov/niosh/hierarchy-of-controls/about
- **Why relevant:** Defines the five-level hierarchy (elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative controls, PPE) used to classify every control and to cap the credit given in residual risk ratings.
- **What was verified:** Page read; the five levels and their order were confirmed.

## R11  PHMSA Safety Advisory: CNG special-permit composite cylinder trailers (rollover risk)
- **Issuer:** US Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration
- **Status:** Voluntary: foreign regulator advisory; recommendations are optional
- **Date:** 12 January 2021
- **Link:** https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/sites/phmsa.dot.gov/files/2021-01/CNG%20Special%20Permit%20Safety%20Advisory.pdf
- **Why relevant:** Reports that CNG trailers with a high centre of gravity roll over more often than most trailers and that driver error featured in the crashes reviewed; recommends driver training on load and centre-of-gravity effects, roll-stability systems and event monitoring. Supports the road-transport hazards H-019 to H-023.
- **What was verified:** Advisory read. It addresses rollover only; it says nothing on inspection, valves, relief devices or loading, so no claims on those are drawn from it.

## R13  Guidelines on occupational safety and health management systems (ILO-OSH 2001)
- **Issuer:** International Labour Organization
- **Status:** Voluntary: international guidance
- **Date:** Published 6 December 2001 (as stated on the ILO page)
- **Link:** https://www.ilo.org/publications/guidelines-occupational-safety-and-health-management-systems-ilo-osh-2001
- **Why relevant:** Practical ILO framework for an OSH management system (policy, planning, implementation, evaluation, improvement). Used alongside ISO 45001 as a second, freely available reference for the plan-do-check-act structure of the action tracker and KPI set.
- **What was verified:** Publication page read for title, date and description. The guideline text itself was not read in full.

## R14  Virtual Pipeline Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) Tube Trailers: emergency response guidance
- **Issuer:** New York State Division of Homeland Security and Emergency Services (DHSES)
- **Status:** Voluntary: foreign government technical guidance (not a Nigerian requirement)
- **Date:** No date printed on the page
- **Link:** https://www.dhses.ny.gov/virtual-pipeline-compressed-natural-gas-cng-tube-trailers
- **Why relevant:** Describes tube-trailer hazards: top-mounted vents that can release gas without warning, releases lasting an hour or longer, rupture and projectile risk, and a 330 ft (about 100 m) initial isolation distance for damaged trailers. Used to justify the exclusion-zone, venting and emergency-response scenarios, not to set Nigerian distances.
- **What was verified:** Page read. The isolation distance is a US emergency-responder figure for a damaged trailer and is not adopted here as a Nigerian or Powergas requirement.

## R15  CGA C-29: Design Requirements for Tube Trailers, Tube Modules, and Multiple Element Gas Containers
- **Issuer:** Compressed Gas Association (CGA)
- **Status:** Voluntary: industry standard (US and Canada regulatory context)
- **Date:** Edition 2, July 2026 (as stated on the CGA catalogue page)
- **Link:** https://legacy.cganet.com/Publication/Details.aspx?id=C-29
- **Why relevant:** Design standard aimed at keeping tube trailers structurally sound in normal handling and reducing tube separation or product release in a highway collision, including rollover. Supports treating trailer structure and rollover protection as matters for competent technical verification (hazards H-020 to H-024).
- **What was verified:** Catalogue page read for title, edition and scope. The standard was not read, and its applicability to trailers used in Nigeria was not assessed.

## Not verified (and therefore not asserted)

- A Nigerian ratification of ILO C155 was not confirmed and is not claimed.
- The Labour Act and other Federal Ministry of Labour guidance were not opened, so they are not cited.
- No public NMDPRA technical standard written specifically for CNG tube-trailer (virtual pipeline) operations was located. Clause-level CNG obligations must be taken from the gazetted regulations and the licence conditions, with legal and technical advice.
- ISO 45001 and NFPA 52 were identified from catalogue pages; neither standard was read in full. Neither is presented as legally required in Nigeria.
- Only the CGA C-29 catalogue page was read; other recognised industrial-gas guidance (for example other CGA or EIGA publications) was not opened and is not cited.
